## Research Findings

### What High-Converting CRE Broker Sites Have
- Hero: clear value prop + single CTA ("View Listings" or "Request Consultation")
- Listings with pro photos, key metrics up front (price/SF, sq ft, cap rate) - no hiding price
- About page (2nd most clicked section - people vet the person)
- Social proof: testimonials, notable deals, transaction count
- Market insights/blog - biggest SEO driver, establishes local authority
- Phone number visible in header on every page
- Booking link (Calendly) to reduce friction
- Gated content (e.g. "Download Q2 Atlanta Commercial Market Report") for email capture

### Key Data Points
- 57% of CRE site traffic comes from organic search - SEO matters from day one
- About page is most visited non-listing page
- Video on landing pages lifts conversions 86% - even a 60-second intro video is high ROI
- Mobile-first critical
- Niche specialization + local market authority is highest-leverage for individual brokers

### Top CRE Broker Site Examples for Reference
- **Richard Bowers & Co. (Atlanta)** - richardbowers.com - Property-type navigation tabs, active listings, team directory
- **BKREA (NYC)** - bkrea.com - "AI-driven brokerage," proprietary map/data tool, newsletter, podcast. Strong personal brand.
- **Madison Partners (LA)** - madisonpartners.net - Boutique-elite positioning, strong principal bio as trust signal
- **TAG (Chicago)** - tagcommercialbroker.com - Tenant-only differentiator, transaction count as trust signal
- **LCRG (Dallas)** - lcrgusa.com - Conflict-free tenant rep messaging, lean and purposeful
